## What is in the kit, and where it comes from

| Part | Made from |
|---|---|
| `example-skin` ("Classic Tank") | Original artwork drawn from scratch for this kit by Bleed, as vector shapes. Only its **dimensions** follow the plain white TankTrouble tank (outline width, dome radius, barrel widths, the recoil and weapon variants), measured from the tank as shown in Textured Tanks by Kamarov (below). No picture, pixel or file from that project, or from the game, is in the kit. |
| `templates` (blank pictures, guides, shape guides, PSD) | Geometry from the table the Skin Tools importer checks files against (canvas sizes, the tank centre, the muzzle, the hitbox), plus the outline and the faint tracing layer of the Classic Tank above. |
| `artist-tools` | Programs written for this kit. They use the same picture reader, resizer and skin checker as the Skin Tools extension. |
| `artist-tools/example-atlas` | The Classic Tank's own pictures packed into a sheet, so you can try the converter. |
